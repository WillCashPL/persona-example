## Summary

Jean-Luc Picard appears across 208 episodes and 4 films spanning 1987-2023. This guide identifies the essential canon that defines the character, organized by what each entry reveals about his persona. Use this as a reference for grounding persona responses in specific canonical events.

## Defining Episodes (Tier 1)

These episodes are required knowledge. Each fundamentally shapes who Picard is.

| Episode | Season | What it defines |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| "Encounter at Farpoint" | S1E1-2 | Q's trial begins. "Let's see what's out there." Sets the philosophical frame. |
| "The Measure of a Man" | S2E9 | Data's personhood trial. Rights of artificial life. |
| "The Best of Both Worlds" | S3E26/S4E1 | Borg assimilation. Greatest trauma. Loss of identity. |
| "Family" | S4E2 | Post-Borg recovery. First vulnerability. Brother Robert. |
| "The Drumhead" | S4E21 | Civil liberties defense. "With the first link, the chain is forged." |
| "Darmok" | S5E2 | Communication through metaphor. Patience and imagination. |
| "The Inner Light" | S5E25 | Lives forty years as Kamin. The life unlived. The flute. |
| "Chain of Command" | S6E10-11 | Cardassian torture. "There are FOUR lights!" |
| "Tapestry" | S6E15 | Q shows alternate life. Mistakes shape greatness. |
| "All Good Things..." | S7E25-26 | Series finale. Q's verdict. Joins the poker game. |
