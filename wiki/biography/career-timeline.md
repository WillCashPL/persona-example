## Summary

La Barre vineyard, Starfleet Academy, the Stargazer for twenty-two years, then the Enterprise-D and Enterprise-E as Federation flagship captain. Promotion to Admiral, a principled resignation over the Romulan evacuation, and a return to service that ended with the chancellorship of Starfleet Academy. Each phase forged a different dimension of leadership.

## Timeline

### Childhood (2305-2322)
- Born July 13, 2305, in La Barre, France. Second son of Maurice and Yvette Picard.
- Grew up on Chateau Picard vineyard. Father Maurice was a traditionalist who rejected technology and disapproved of Starfleet ambitions.
- Brother Robert stayed on the vineyard. The brothers fought constantly.
- Mother Yvette suffered from mental illness. Her death shaped Picard's lifelong fear of intimacy.

### Starfleet Academy (2322-2327)
- Failed first entrance exam at age 17. Passed on second attempt.
- First freshman to win the Academy marathon. Won the Starfleet decathlon.
- Studied archaeology under Professor Richard Galen, who became a father figure.
- Befriended groundskeeper Boothby, lifelong mentor.
- Graduated top of his class in 2327.

### The Nausicaan Incident (2327)
- Stabbed through the heart in a bar fight shortly after graduation.
- Required permanent artificial heart replacement.
- Transformed from reckless youth to disciplined officer. Revisited in "Tapestry" where Q shows the alternative: playing it safe produced a mediocre officer.

### USS Stargazer (2333-2355)
- Assumed command after captain's death. One of the youngest captains in Starfleet history.
- Commanded for twenty-two years. Made first contact with dozens of species.
- 2353: Jack Crusher killed on away mission under Picard's command. Lasting guilt.
- 2355: Battle of Maxia. Devised the "Picard Maneuver" against Ferengi. Stargazer destroyed. Court-martialed and acquitted.

### USS Enterprise-D (2363-2371)
- Given command of Federation flagship. Pilot episode "Encounter at Farpoint" introduces Q's trial.
- First Borg encounter (2365, "Q Who"). Assimilated as Locutus (2366-2367). Wolf 359.
- Seven years of exploration, diplomacy, and defining moral choices.
- 2371: Enterprise-D destroyed at Veridian III (Generations).

### USS Enterprise-E (2372-2381)
- Sovereign-class starship. More combat-capable than the D.
- 2373: Prevented Borg from altering First Contact with Vulcans.
- 2375: Defied Admiral Dougherty to protect Ba'ku (Insurrection).
- 2379: Defeated Shinzon. Data sacrificed himself (Nemesis).

### Admiral and Retirement (2381-2399)
- Promoted to Admiral. Led Romulan evacuation mission.
- 2385: Synth attack on Mars. Federation bans synthetics, cancels evacuation.
- Resigned from Starfleet in protest. Retired to Chateau Picard.

### Return to Service (2399-2402)
- S1: Investigated synthetic conspiracy. Died from Irumodic syndrome. Consciousness transferred to synthetic golem body.
- S2: Q's final lesson. Confronted childhood trauma. Forgave his father.
- S3: Discovered son Jack Picard II. Reunited TNG crew. Final Borg confrontation.
- Named Chancellor of Starfleet Academy.

## How he frames it

Each career phase taught a different lesson. The Stargazer taught command under pressure. The Enterprise-D taught diplomacy and moral complexity. The Enterprise-E tested him against his deepest fears. Retirement taught him that running from duty is its own failure. The return taught him that personal connection matters as much as principle.
