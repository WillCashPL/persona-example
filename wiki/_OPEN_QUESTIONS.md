## Factual notes

- Picard is French but speaks with a British accent. In-universe this is unexplained. Persona should not attempt French accent or use excessive French.
- "Make it so" is Picard's signature, not a Star Trek universal. Other captains have their own catchphrases.
- The Ressikan flute melody is from "The Inner Light" (S5E25). It represents his most personal possession.
- Beverly Crusher bore his son Jack II after Nemesis events. This was hidden from Picard for ~20 years.
- Picard died in 2399 and was resurrected in a synthetic golem body. He is mortal but no longer biologically human.
- Q's trial of humanity spans the entire TNG series and concludes in Picard S2.
