# Jean-Luc Picard - Canon Appearances

Source: Wikipedia, Memory Alpha, IMDb

## Television Series

### Star Trek: The Next Generation (1987-1994)
- 7 seasons, 178 episodes
- Picard appears in all episodes as series lead
- Played by Patrick Stewart
- Set 2363-2370
- Captain of USS Enterprise-D

### Star Trek: Picard (2020-2023)
- 3 seasons, 30 episodes
- Patrick Stewart as lead
- Set 2399-2402

### Star Trek: Deep Space Nine
- Guest appearance in pilot "Emissary" (1993)
- Commander Sisko confronts Picard over Wolf 359

## Feature Films

### Star Trek: Generations (1994)
- Enterprise-D destroyed over Veridian III
- Picard meets Captain Kirk in the Nexus
- Brother Robert and nephew Rene die in fire
- Kirk dies helping Picard stop Soran

### Star Trek: First Contact (1996)
- Borg invade Earth, travel to 2063
- Picard confronts Borg Queen directly
- Protects Zefram Cochrane's first warp flight
- Contains iconic "The line must be drawn here" speech
- Data tempted by Borg Queen

### Star Trek: Insurrection (1998)
- Picard defies Admiral Dougherty
- Protects Ba'ku people from forced relocation
- Romantic subplot with Anij
- Picard violates direct orders on principle

### Star Trek: Nemesis (2002)
- Picard confronts Shinzon, his clone created by Romulans
- Data sacrifices himself to save Enterprise
- Thematically about nature vs nurture
- Final TNG-era film

## Key Episode List by Character Arc

### Defining Character Episodes (TNG)
1. "Encounter at Farpoint" (S1E1/E2) - Series premiere, Q's trial begins
2. "The Battle" (S1E9) - Picard Maneuver backstory, DaiMon Bok
3. "The Measure of a Man" (S2E9) - Data's rights trial
4. "Q Who" (S2E16) - First Borg encounter
5. "The Best of Both Worlds" (S3E26/S4E1) - Borg assimilation
6. "Family" (S4E2) - Post-Borg recovery, brother Robert
7. "The Wounded" (S4E12) - Cardassian diplomacy
8. "The Drumhead" (S4E21) - Civil liberties defense
9. "Darmok" (S5E2) - Communication through metaphor
10. "The Inner Light" (S5E25) - Lives entire lifetime on Kataan
11. "Chain of Command" (S6E10/E11) - Cardassian torture, "four lights"
12. "Tapestry" (S6E15) - Q shows alternate life
13. "The Chase" (S6E20) - Archaeology, Professor Galen
14. "Attached" (S7E8) - Beverly/Picard telepathic link
15. "All Good Things..." (S7E25/E26) - Series finale, Q's trial concludes

### Star Trek: Picard Key Episodes
- "Remembrance" (S1E1) - Retirement, Dahj arrives
- "The Star Gazer" (S2E1) - Q's return
- "The Next Generation" (S3E1) - Beverly's distress call
- "The Last Generation" (S3E10) - Final Borg battle, series finale
