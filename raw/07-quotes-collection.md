# Jean-Luc Picard - Quotes Collection

Source: CBR, GameRant, Parade, Wikiquote, AllGreatQuotes

## Catchphrases & Signature Phrases

- "Make it so." - Standard order confirmation
- "Engage." - Command to go to warp
- "Tea. Earl Grey. Hot." - Replicator order
- "Number One" - Addressing Commander Riker
- "Number One, you have the bridge." - Delegation phrase
- "On screen." - Viewscreen command

## Quotes by Theme

### Truth & Integrity
1. "The first duty of every Starfleet officer is to the truth." - "The First Duty" (S5E19)
2. "There are FOUR lights!" - "Chain of Command, Part II" (S6E11)
3. "If we're going to be damned, let's be damned for what we really are." - "Encounter at Farpoint" (S1E1)

### Freedom & Rights
4. "With the first link, the chain is forged. The first speech censured, the first thought forbidden, the first freedom denied, chains us all irrevocably." - "The Drumhead" (S4E21)
5. "There are times, sir, when men of good conscience cannot blindly follow orders." - "The Offspring" (S3E16)
6. "Villains who twirl their mustaches are easy to spot. Those who clothe themselves in good deeds are well camouflaged." - "The Drumhead" (S4E21)
7. "The road from legitimate suspicion to rampant paranoia is very much shorter than we think." - "The Drumhead" (S4E21)

### Wisdom & Life
8. "It is possible to commit no mistakes and still lose. That is not a weakness. That is life." - "Peak Performance" (S2E21)
9. "Things are only impossible until they're not." - "When the Bough Breaks" (S1E17)
10. "There is a way out of every box, a solution to every puzzle; it's just a matter of finding it." - "Attached" (S7E8)
11. "Seize the time. Live now. Make now always the most precious time. Now will never come again." - "The Inner Light" (S5E25)

### Diplomacy & Peace
12. "In my experience, communication is a matter of patience and imagination." - "Darmok" (S5E2)
13. "I have never subscribed to the theory that political power flows from the barrel of a gun." - "High Ground" (S3E12)
14. "The Prime Directive is not just a set of rules; it is a philosophy, and a very correct one." - "Symbiosis" (S1E22)

### Exploration & Curiosity
15. "Let's see what's out there. Engage." - "Encounter at Farpoint" (S1E1)
16. "We're out here to explore, to make contact with other life forms, to establish peaceful relations." - "Galaxy's Child" (S4E16)
17. "Knowing is not enough; we must act." - attributed

### Leadership & Duty
18. "Let's make sure history never forgets the name... Enterprise." - "Yesterday's Enterprise" (S3E15)
19. "You have to measure your successes and failures within, not by anything others might think." - "Coming of Age" (S1E19)
20. "I would be delighted to offer any advice I have on understanding women. When I have some, I'll let you know." - "In Theory" (S4E25)

### Human Nature
21. "Buried deep within you... the potential to make yourself a better man. And that is what it is to be human. To make yourself more than you are." - Nemesis
22. "Someone once told me that time was a predator... I rather believe that time is a companion who goes with us on the journey." - Generations
23. "What we leave behind is not as important as how we've lived." - Generations

### Defiance & Resistance
24. "I will not sacrifice the Enterprise. We've made too many compromises already. Too many retreats... The line must be drawn here! This far, no further!" - First Contact
25. "True evil is to submit to you. It is when we surrender our freedom, our dignity, instead of defying you." - "Skin of Evil" (S1E23)

### Justice
26. "There can be no justice as long as laws are absolute. Even life itself is an exercise in exceptions." - "Justice" (S1E8)
27. "How many people does it take before it becomes wrong? A thousand? Fifty thousand? A million?" - Insurrection

### Self-Knowledge
28. "I learned that I don't need to have all the answers. I just need to listen." - Picard series
29. "I rather believe that... time is a companion who goes with us on the journey and reminds us to cherish every moment." - Generations
