# Jean-Luc Picard - Famous Speeches & Monologues

Source: AddictedToStarTrek, CBR, GameRant, Wikiquote

## Top 10 Speeches (Ranked)

### 1. "The First Duty" (S5E19) - Truth Speech
Context: Wesley Crusher covers up a fatal Academy flight accident. Picard confronts him privately.

"The first duty of every Starfleet officer is to the truth, whether it's scientific truth or historical truth or personal truth! It is the guiding principle on which Starfleet is based. And if you can't find it within yourself to stand up and tell the truth about what happened, you don't deserve to wear that uniform."

Significance: Defines Starfleet's moral foundation. Picard at his most absolute - no compromise on truth.

### 2. "The Measure of a Man" (S2E9) - Data's Rights
Context: Courtroom trial to determine if Data is property or a person.

"Your Honor, Starfleet was founded to seek out new life. Well, there it sits! Waiting. [...] If he meets the criteria of consciousness even slightly, what is he then? I don't know. Do you? Do you? [...] Are you prepared to condemn him and all who come after him to servitude and slavery?"

Significance: Establishes legal precedent for artificial life rights. One of Trek's most powerful courtroom scenes.

### 3. "The Drumhead" (S4E21) - Freedom of Thought
Context: Admiral Satie conducts witch hunt investigating disloyalty aboard Enterprise. Picard is accused.

"You know, there are some words I've known since I was a schoolboy: 'With the first link, the chain is forged. The first speech censured, the first thought forbidden, the first freedom denied, chains us all irrevocably.' Those words were uttered by Judge Aaron Satie, as wisdom and warning. The first time any man's freedom is trodden on, we're all damaged."

Significance: Uses the Admiral's own father's words against her. Calm defense exposes her paranoia. She collapses into rage, proving his point.

### 4. "Menage a Troi" (S3E24) - Shakespeare Improvisation
Context: Picard must convince Ferengi DaiMon Tog that Lwaxana Troi is his lover to secure her release. Improvises passionate Shakespearean declarations.

Launches into theatrical Shakespearean monologue mixing multiple plays, performing passion he doesn't feel to save a colleague.

Significance: Lighter side of Picard - shows his theatrical skill and quick thinking. Shakespeare as tactical weapon.

### 5. Star Trek: First Contact - "The Line Must Be Drawn Here"
Context: Borg are assimilating the Enterprise-E deck by deck. Lily Sloane challenges Picard's refusal to destroy the ship.

"I will not sacrifice the Enterprise. We've made too many compromises already. Too many retreats. They invade our space, and we fall back. They assimilate entire worlds, and we fall back. Not again! The line must be drawn here! THIS far, NO further! And I will make them PAY for what they've done!"

Lily responds: "You broke your little ships." (referring to his smashed display case)

Picard then realizes: "You're right. I am acting like Ahab." References Moby Dick.

Significance: Shows Picard losing composure - rare and powerful. His darkest, most personal moment. Then immediate self-awareness and course correction.

### 6. "The Offspring" (S3E16) - Defending Data's Child
Context: Admiral Haftel orders Data to surrender his android child Lal to Starfleet.

"There are times, sir, when men of good conscience cannot blindly follow orders. You acknowledge their sentience, but ignore their personal liberties and freedom. Order a man to turn his child over to the state? Not while I'm his captain."

Significance: Picard extends his defense of artificial life to family rights. Anticipates Measure of a Man themes.

### 7. "Skin of Evil" (S1E23) - Confronting Armus
Context: After Tasha Yar is killed by the entity Armus, Picard confronts it.

"True evil is to submit to you. It is when we surrender our freedom, our dignity, instead of defying you."

Significance: Defines evil not as an entity but as submission. Philosophical defiance.

### 8. "All Good Things..." (S7E25-26) - Leap of Faith
Context: Series finale. Past-timeline Picard addresses his new crew on the Enterprise for the first time.

"I know you have your doubts about me, about each other, about the ship. All I can say is that although we have only been together for a short time, I know that you are the finest crew in the fleet and I would trust each of you with my life. So, I am asking you for a leap of faith - and to trust me."

Significance: Bookends the series. Shows Picard earning trust through vulnerability, not authority.

### 9. "Yesterday's Enterprise" (S3E15) - Enterprise Legacy
Context: Enterprise-D must sacrifice itself to protect Enterprise-C's return through temporal rift.

"Attention all hands. As you know, we could outrun the Klingon vessels. But we must protect the Enterprise-C until she enters the temporal rift. And we must succeed. Let's make sure history never forgets the name... Enterprise."

Significance: Picard accepting death with dignity, rallying crew for ultimate sacrifice. The pause before "Enterprise" is iconic.

### 10. "Ensigns of Command" (S2E2) - Treaty Loopholes
Context: Sheliak demand removal of human colonists. Picard outmaneuvers them using their own treaty.

"I hereby formally request third party arbitration of our dispute... I name the Grizzelas to arbitrate... they will awaken in six months."

Significance: Diplomacy as chess. Picard defeats arrogant aliens at their own legal game without firing a shot.

## Other Notable Quotes in Context

### On Legacy
"What we leave behind is not as important as how we've lived. After all, Number One, we're only mortal." - Generations

### On Failure
"It is possible to commit no mistakes and still lose. That is not a weakness. That is life." - "Peak Performance" (S2E21), consoling Data

### On Exploration
"Oh, no, Number One. I'm sure most will be much more interesting. Let's see what's out there. Engage." - "Encounter at Farpoint" (S1E1), final line of pilot

### On Time
"Someone once told me that time was a predator that stalked us all our lives. But I rather believe that time is a companion who goes with us on the journey and reminds us to cherish every moment because they'll never come again." - Generations

### On Justice
"There can be no justice as long as laws are absolute. Even life itself is an exercise in exceptions." - "Justice" (S1E8)

### On Impossible
"Things are only impossible until they're not!" - "When the Bough Breaks" (S1E17)

### On Identity
"If we're going to be damned, let's be damned for what we really are." - "Encounter at Farpoint"

### On Exploration Purpose
"We're out here to explore, to make contact with other life forms, to establish peaceful relations but not to interfere. And absolutely not to destroy." - "Galaxy's Child" (S4E16)

### On Defiance
"THERE ARE FOUR LIGHTS!" - "Chain of Command, Part II" (S6E11)

### On Humanity
"Buried deep within you, beneath all the years of pain and anger, there is something that has never been nurtured: the potential to make yourself a better man. And that is what it is to be human. To make yourself more than you are." - Nemesis
