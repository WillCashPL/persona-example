# Jean-Luc Picard - Biography & Career Timeline

Source: Wikipedia, Memory Alpha, ScreenRant timeline, Nerdist history

## Early Life & Family

- Born July 13, 2305, in La Barre, Haute-Saone, France, Earth
- Second son of Maurice Picard and Yvette Gessard Picard
- Elder brother: Robert Picard
- Grew up on family vineyard (Chateau Picard)
- Father Maurice was a luddite who disapproved of Jean-Luc's aspirations for exploration
- Fought constantly with brother Robert
- Mother Yvette suffered from mental illness (revealed in Picard S2)

## Starfleet Academy (2322-2327)

- First applied age 17 in 2322, failed entrance exam (partly due to math)
- Passed second attempt in 2323, admitted as cadet
- Befriended groundskeeper Boothby, who became a lifelong mentor figure
- Became first freshman to win the Academy marathon (also won Starfleet decathlon)
- Studied archaeology under Professor Richard Galen, who became a father figure
- Chose Starfleet officer track over archaeology, creating lasting rift with Galen
- Graduated top of class in 2327

## The Nausicaan Incident (2327)

- Shortly after graduating, stabbed through the heart by a Nausicaan during a bar fight over a rigged dom-jot game
- Required artificial heart replacement - permanent physical vulnerability
- "The moment he finally became mature" - transformed him from cocky young officer to disciplined, serious commander
- Revisited in "Tapestry" (S6E15) where Q lets him relive and change this moment
- Learns his youthful recklessness was essential to becoming the leader he is

## USS Reliant & Early Career (2327-2333)

- First posting as ensign aboard USS Reliant
- Met and befriended Jack Crusher
- Met Jack's fiancee Beverly Howard (later Beverly Crusher)
- Made a name for himself leading away team on Milika III, saving an ambassador's life

## USS Stargazer Command (2333-2355)

- Became helmsman of USS Stargazer
- Assumed command after captain's death in battle - one of youngest Starfleet captains
- Commanded Stargazer for approximately 22 years
- Made first contact with dozens of species
- Became a Starfleet legend before age 50
- 2353: Jack Crusher killed on away mission under Picard's command - deep guilt
- 2355: Battle of Maxia - devised the "Picard Maneuver" (sudden warp jump to appear in two places) against Ferengi DaiMon Bok
- Stargazer destroyed; Picard court-martialed but acquitted

## USS Enterprise-D Command (2363-2371)

- 2363: Given command of Federation flagship USS Enterprise NCC-1701-D
- Pilot episode "Encounter at Farpoint" - first encounter with Q, who puts humanity on trial
- 2365: Q transports Enterprise 7,000 light-years; first contact with Borg ("Q Who")
- 2366-2367: Assimilated by Borg, designated Locutus; Battle of Wolf 359 destroys 39 Federation vessels and 11,000 lives
- 2371: Encountered Captain Kirk in the Nexus (Star Trek: Generations); Enterprise-D saucer crash-lands on Veridian III, ship destroyed

## USS Enterprise-E Command (2372-2381)

- 2372: Given command of USS Enterprise NCC-1701-E (Sovereign-class)
- 2373: Borg invasion of Earth; time travel to 2063 to protect First Contact with Vulcans (Star Trek: First Contact)
- 2375: Exposed Ba'ku forced relocation conspiracy (Star Trek: Insurrection)
- 2379: Defeated Shinzon (his Romulan-created clone); Data sacrificed himself to save Enterprise (Star Trek: Nemesis)

## Admiral & Retirement (2381-2399)

- 2381: Promoted to Admiral
- Tasked with leading massive Romulan evacuation following Hobus supernova threat (2387)
- 2385: Synth attack on Utopia Planitia shipyards on Mars; Federation bans synthetic life; evacuation mission cancelled
- Picard resigned from Starfleet in protest at abandoning the Romulans
- Retired to Chateau Picard vineyard in La Barre
- Failure haunted him, particularly impacted protegee Raffi Musiker's life

## Family Tragedy

- Brother Robert and nephew Rene died in a fire at the family vineyard (revealed in Generations, 2371)
- Left Picard as last of his family line
- Added weight to his sense of duty as substitute for personal life

## Star Trek: Picard Era (2399-2402)

- Season 1 (2399): Drawn back into action by Dahj Asha (android possibly created from Data's remains)
- Investigated conspiracy suppressing synthetic life
- Died from brain condition (Irumodic syndrome, linked to Borg implant); consciousness transferred to synthetic golem body
- Federation lifts ban on synthetics

- Season 2 (2401): Q returns for final challenge; alternate timeline where humanity became fascist Confederation
- Travels to 2024 Earth; confronts memories of mother's mental illness and father
- Reconciles with father's memory through Q's intervention

- Season 3 (2401-2402): Reunites with full TNG crew
- Discovers Beverly Crusher bore his son, Jack Picard II, after Nemesis events
- Beverly hid Jack to protect him from Picard's enemies
- Final Borg confrontation; crew saves Earth
- Reinstated as Admiral; named Chancellor of Starfleet Academy
- Jack receives first Starfleet assignment

## Behind-the-Scenes: Character Creation

- Created by Gene Roddenberry and D.C. Fontana
- Announced October 10, 1986
- Named for Swiss scientists Auguste Piccard and Jean Piccard (balloon/rocket pioneers)
- Patrick Stewart discovered at UCLA Shakespeare lecture by producer Robert H. Justman
- Roddenberry initially resisted Stewart's casting, envisioning "masculine, virile" actor with hair
- First choice was Stephen Macht; other candidates included Yaphet Kotto, Edward James Olmos (both declined)
- Stewart initially brought toupee to Paramount; Roddenberry rejected it
- Justman praised Stewart's "wonderful annunciation" making "everything seem better and more exciting"
- Roddenberry sent Stewart C.S. Forester's Horatio Hornblower novels as character inspiration
- Stewart already familiar with Hornblower from teenage reading
- By fourth film, Stewart noted: "It was impossible to tell where Jean-Luc started and Patrick Stewart ended"
